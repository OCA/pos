This module allows to mark a POS order as fully paid while writing off
the difference to a pre-defined account.

A new "Mark as Paid" button is displayed on the POS payment screen. You
configure the write-off thresholds per Point of Sale: a percentage of
the order total and an amount. An order can be marked as paid only if
the write-off amount does not exceed any of these thresholds.

The write-off is shown on the receipt, on the POS order, in the session
report, and as a separate line on the invoice of an invoiced order. When
the session is closed, the write-offs of the orders that are not
invoiced are booked on the write-off account.

Use Cases / Context
===================

Sometimes a cash POS order has to be rounded because there is no change,
or because the residual amount is insignificant.

For example, a customer wants to pay an order of 5,000.07 in cash, but
has only 1,000, 500 and 100 bills and no coins.

Configuration
=============

**Turn on Mark as Paid**

1. Open the **Point of Sale** app.
2. Click **Configuration**, then **Settings**.
3. In **Point of Sale**, select the Point of Sale to configure.
4. In **Payment**, turn on **Mark as Paid**.
5. In **Write-off Account**, select the account that receives the
   written-off amounts.
6. In **Max Write-off (%)**, type the highest write-off allowed, in
   percent of the order total including taxes. Type 0 for no limit.
7. In **Max Write-off Amount**, type the highest write-off allowed, as
   an amount. Type 0 for no limit.
8. In **Receipt Label**, type the label printed on the receipt and on
   the invoice, e.g. "Extra discount".
9. Click **Save**.

Notes:

- At least one of **Max Write-off (%)** and **Max Write-off Amount**
  must be more than 0. When both are set, the write-off must not exceed
  either of them.
- The Point of Sale needs a cash payment method: an order can be marked
  as paid only when it has a cash payment.
- The settings cannot be changed while a session of the Point of Sale is
  open. Close the session first.

Usage
=====

**Mark an order as paid**

1. Open the **Point of Sale** app.
2. On the Point of Sale, click **New Session** or **Continue Selling**.
3. Add the products to the order.
4. Click **Payment**.
5. Click the cash payment method.
6. Type the amount received from the customer.
7. Click **Mark as Paid**. The button is highlighted.
8. Check the **Write-off** row: it shows the amount that is written off.
   **Remaining** shows 0.00.
9. Click **Validate**.

The receipt shows the write-off with the receipt label of the Point of
Sale, and the amount to pay after the write-off.

**Unmark an order**

1. Click **Mark as Paid** again. The button is no longer highlighted.

Changing a product line or a payment line also unmarks the order. Click
**Mark as Paid** again after the change.

**When Mark as Paid is refused**

A message explains why the order cannot be marked:

- the order total is 0 or negative;
- nothing is left to pay;
- the order has no cash payment;
- the order has a **Customer Account** payment;
- the amount left to pay exceeds **Max Write-off Amount** or **Max
  Write-off (%)**;
- a payment on a payment terminal is not finished. Finish or remove it
  first.

Known issues / Roadmap
======================

- Refunds and negative orders are not supported. A refund of a
  written-off order refunds the full price, including the written-off
  amount.
- On a written-off order, **Total Paid** in the backend includes the
  write-off. See **Write-off Amount** for the part not received.
- The sale details printed from the POS do not show the write-off. The
  session report printed from the backend shows it.
- Interaction with ``pos_multi_currency`` is not supported or tested.
