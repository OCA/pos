**Turn on Mark as Paid**

1. Open the **Point of Sale** app.
2. Click **Configuration**, then **Settings**.
3. In **Point of Sale**, select the Point of Sale to configure.
4. In **Payment**, turn on **Mark as Paid**.
5. In **Write-off Account**, select the account that receives the written-off amounts.
6. In **Max Write-off (%)**, type the highest write-off allowed, in percent of the
   order total including taxes. Type 0 for no limit.
7. In **Max Write-off Amount**, type the highest write-off allowed, as an amount.
   Type 0 for no limit.
8. In **Receipt Label**, type the label printed on the receipt and on the invoice,
   e.g. "Extra discount".
9. Click **Save**.

Notes:

- At least one of **Max Write-off (%)** and **Max Write-off Amount** must be more
  than 0. When both are set, the write-off must not exceed either of them.
- The Point of Sale needs a cash payment method: an order can be marked as paid only
  when it has a cash payment.
- The settings cannot be changed while a session of the Point of Sale is open.
  Close the session first.
