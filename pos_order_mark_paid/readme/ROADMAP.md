- Refunds and negative orders are not supported. A refund of a written-off order
  refunds the full price, including the written-off amount.
- On a written-off order, **Total Paid** in the backend includes the write-off. See
  **Write-off Amount** for the part not received.
- The sale details printed from the POS do not show the write-off. The session report
  printed from the backend shows it.
- Interaction with `pos_multi_currency` is not supported or tested.
