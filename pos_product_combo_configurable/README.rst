This module allows configuring and selling customizable product combos and packs in the Point of Sale (POS) interface.

**Key Features**:

* **Combo Product Configuration**: Mark any product with **Use as Combo** to configure its component rules inside the **Combo Configuration** tab.
* **Mandatory & Optional Categories**: Define **Required Products** (mandatory selection) and optional add-on categories grouped by POS Category.
* **Automatic or Manual Product Selection**: Automatically load all POS-available products from a category using **Include all Products**, or manually pick specific products.
* **Flexible Pricing Rules**:

  * When **Include in Main Product Price** is enabled, the first selected product on that line (up to its **Default Quantity**) is included at no extra cost in the combo base price, while any additional selected product or quantity above the default adds to the **Extra Price**.
  * When **Include in Main Product Price** is disabled, every selected item and unit adds its price to the **Extra Price**.

* **Selection Limits & Default Quantities**: Configure **Default Quantity** and maximum selectable **No. of Items** per category line, with backend validation preventing invalid quantities or duplicate optional categories.
* **Modern POS Interface**:

  * **COMBO** badge pill on combo product cards in the POS product grid.
  * **Configure Your Combo** modal divided into **Mandatory Selection** and **Customize & Extras** sections, with per-category search (**Filter category...**), selection counters, quantity steppers (``-`` / ``+``), and live **Base Price**, **+ Extra**, and **Add to Order** total calculation.
  * Configurable product thumbnails in the popup via **View Combo Product Image** in POS settings.
  * Direct **Edit** button and **Combo Breakdown** list on POS cart orderlines.

* **End-to-End Traceability (Receipt, Order, Stock & Invoice)**:

  * Displays selected combo items on the POS receipt.
  * Automatically expands combo sub-products into POS Order lines with ``0.00`` unit price (marked with **Combo Item**) so stock pickings deduct component inventory accurately.
  * Formats combo sub-lines cleanly on printed customer invoices with an indented ``↳`` indicator and **Combo Item** badge.

Configuration
=============

To configure this module, you need to:

1. Go to **Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings** (or the settings of a specific Point of Sale), locate the **POS Combo Packs** section, and enable or disable **View Combo Product Image** depending on whether you want sub-product thumbnails shown in the POS combo popup.
2. Go to **Point of Sale > Products > Products** and create or open the main combo product.
3. Enable the **Use as Combo** checkbox under the product title.
4. Open the **Combo Configuration** tab and add one or more category lines:

   * **Category**: Select the POS Category for the line.
   * **Required Products**: Enable if the cashier/customer must keep at least one item selected from this line. When opening the combo popup in POS, only the **first product** of each required line is pre-selected by default.
   * **Include in Main Product Price**:

     * If enabled, the first selected product on this line (up to **Default Quantity**) is included in the combo's base price; selecting a second product or increasing the quantity above **Default Quantity** adds an **Extra Price**.
     * If disabled, any selected product on this line adds its unit price multiplied by the selected quantity to the **Extra Price**.

   * **Include all Products**: Enable to automatically populate all POS-available non-combo products belonging to the selected **Category**, or leave disabled to choose specific products manually.
   * **Product**: Select the allowed sub-products for this category line.
   * **Default Quantity**: Set the initial quantity assigned when a product is selected (must be at least ``1``).
   * **No. of Items**: Set the maximum total number of items/units that can be selected in this category line (whether picking different products or multiple units of the same product with ``+``).

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

1. Open a Point of Sale session.
2. Identify combo products in the product grid by the **COMBO** badge in the top-left corner of the product card.
3. Click a combo product to open the **Configure Your Combo** popup:

   * **Mandatory Selection**:

     * On each required category line, the first product is pre-selected with its **Default Quantity**.
     * If **No. of Items** is ``1``, clicking another product in the same category automatically switches the selection to that product.
     * If **No. of Items** is greater than ``1``, you can select additional products up to the allowed limit (any additional product beyond the first on an included-price line will add to the **+ Extra** price).
     * Attempting to confirm without at least one selected item in every required line displays an alert.

   * **Customize & Extras**:

     * In optional categories, click any product card to select or deselect up to **No. of Items** products.

   * **Quantities & Filtering**:

     * Use the ``-`` and ``+`` stepper buttons on any selected product card to adjust its quantity up to the category's **No. of Items** limit (units above **Default Quantity** on included-price lines, or any unit on non-included lines, add to **+ Extra**).
     * Use the **Search Products...** search box in each category header to quickly locate products.

   * **Confirmation**:

     * Check the live **Base Price** and **+ Extra** chips in the header and click **Add to Order · <Total>** in the footer.

4. **Review & Edit in the Cart**:

   * In the POS cart, the combo line displays a **Combo Breakdown** with all selected sub-products and their quantities.
   * Click the **Edit** button on the combo orderline at any time to reopen the **Configure Your Combo** popup and modify products or quantities.

5. **Validate the Order**:

   * **POS Receipt**: Shows the main combo product and its **Included Items** breakdown.
   * **Backend POS Order**: Creates the main combo order line (with the final combo price) followed by individual sub-product lines marked as **Combo Item** with ``0.00`` unit price.
   * **Stock Picking**: Automatically deducts inventory for all selected sub-products.
   * **Invoice**: When invoiced, sub-products appear grouped under the combo with a ``↳`` indicator and a **Combo Item** badge on the printed invoice report.
