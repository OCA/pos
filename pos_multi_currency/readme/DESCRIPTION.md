Take Point of Sale payments in foreign currencies when the Point of Sale
uses the company currency.

- The cashier types the amount received in the foreign currency and picks
  that currency on the payment line.
- The Point of Sale amount is converted at the company rate of the payment
  day. Order prices stay in the Point of Sale currency.
- The session books the tender in the foreign currency and posts the
  exchange difference when the receivable is reconciled.
- One order can mix cash and card tenders in several currencies.
- Closing differences for foreign card payments are posted per payment
  method and currency. Cash differences stay on the session cash register,
  in each currency.
