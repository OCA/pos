POS Multi Currency

Take Point of Sale payments in foreign currencies when the Point of Sale uses the company currency.

  • The cashier types the amount received in the foreign currency and picks that currency on the payment line.
  • The Point of Sale amount is converted at the company rate of the payment day. Order prices stay in the Point of Sale currency.
  • The session books the tender in the foreign currency and posts the exchange difference when the receivable is reconciled.
  • One order can mix cash and card tenders in several currencies.
  • Closing differences for foreign card payments are posted per payment method and currency. Cash differences stay on the session cash register, in each currency.

Configuration

Activate the foreign currency

Who: Billing Administrator.

  1. Open the Invoicing app.
  2. Click Configuration, then Accounting, then Currencies.
  3. Open the foreign currency.
  4. Turn on Active.
  5. Click Save.

A second active currency turns on Multi-Currencies for every internal user.

Set exchange difference accounts

Who: a user in Settings. The block appears only when Multi-Currencies is on.

  1. Open the Invoicing app.
  2. Click Configuration, then Settings.
  3. In Default Accounts, under Post Exchange difference entries in:, set Journal, Gain and Loss.
  4. Click Save.

A session that took a foreign tender cannot close while any of these is empty.

Set cash and bank difference accounts

Who: Billing Administrator.

  1. Open the Invoicing app.
  2. Click Configuration, then Accounting, then Journals.
  3. Open the cash journal used by the Point of Sale cash method.
  4. Open the Journal Entries tab.
  5. Set Profit Account and Loss Account.
  6. Click Save.
  7. Repeat steps 3 to 6 for each bank journal used by a payment method.

Foreign cash and card closing differences use these accounts.

Enable foreign currencies on the Point of Sale

Who: a Point of Sale manager who also has Settings. The Point of Sale currency must be the company currency.

  1. Open the Point of Sale app.
  2. Click Configuration, then Settings.
  3. In the Payment section, set Foreign Currencies. The company currency and the Point of Sale currency are not offered. Leave the list empty to take payments in the Point of Sale currency only.
  4. Click Save.

The list cannot be changed while a session of that Point of Sale is open. A currency cannot be removed while its last counted closing is not zero.

Optional: bank currency difference journal

Who: a Point of Sale manager.

  1. Open the Point of Sale app.
  2. Click Configuration, then Payment Methods.
  3. Open a bank method.
  4. Set Currency Difference Journal when a foreign card closing difference must be posted in another journal. Leave it empty to use the method’s own journal.
  5. Click Save.

Cash differences, and differences in the Point of Sale currency, stay on the method’s journal.

Usage

Open a session

  1. Open the Point of Sale app.
  2. Open a Point of Sale that has foreign currencies.
  3. On Opening Cash Control, type the counted cash in the Point of Sale currency.
  4. For each Opening foreign-currency field, type the counted amount. The proposed value is the counted closing of the previous session.
  5. Click Open session.
  6. The difference, if any, is posted on the cash journal in that currency.

Take a payment in a foreign currency

  1. On the payment screen, click a cash or bank payment method.
  2. On the keypad, type the amount the customer hands over in the foreign currency (for example 500 for 500 euro).
  3. Click Currency.
  4. Click the currency. The rate is shown as 1 <POS currency> = <rate> <foreign currency> (for example 1 USD = 0.227273 F).
  5. Check Amount received: it is the number from step 2, in that currency. Point of Sale amount is that number converted at the rate. Amount due before this payment is the amount due in that currency before the selected payment line.
  6. If you skipped step 2, Amount received starts as the amount still due in that currency. Change it in the field when needed.
  7. Click Apply.
  8. Click Validate.

Enter a foreign amount on a Point of Sale with no cash method

The payment-screen keypad rejects amounts above the Point of Sale amount due.

  1. On the payment screen, click a bank payment method.
  2. Click Currency.
  3. Click the foreign currency.
  4. Type the foreign amount in Amount received.
  5. Click Apply.
  6. Click Validate.

Change or clear a foreign tender

  1. Select the payment line.
  2. Type a new amount on the keypad. The first number key starts a new foreign amount; following digits continue that number.
  3. To add foreign units instead, click +10, +20 or +50.
  4. To set another amount: click Currency, change Amount received, then click Apply.
  5. To drop the foreign currency and keep the Point of Sale amount: click Currency, then Use POS currency.
  6. To leave the line unchanged: click Currency, then Cancel.

When Amount received equals the amount still due in that currency, the order settles with no change. A larger amount, including one cent more, is an overpayment: change is given in the Point of Sale currency on the cash register.

Currency is hidden when the Point of Sale has no foreign currencies. It stays disabled for customer-account lines, a second cash method, a payment terminal, and the change line.

Put foreign cash in or take it out

Cash In/Out is shown only with cash control (a cash payment method) and Invoicing: Billing access (Accounting: Billing in Enterprise), or a role that includes it. If the menu is missing, ask your POS manager to check the cash payment method and your administrator to check your access.

  1. In the session menu, click Cash In/Out.
  2. Click Cash In or Cash Out.
  3. Choose the currency in the list.
  4. Type the amount.
  5. Type the Reason.
  6. Click Confirm. There is no limit on the amount taken out.

Refund in a foreign currency

  1. Pay the refund with a foreign cash tender as above.
  2. If the payout is larger than that currency’s drawer (including orders not yet sent to the server), a warning appears.
  3. Click Ok to continue, or Cancel to return to the payment screen. The server does not block the refund. The refund uses the rate of the refund day.

Close a session

  1. In the session menu, click Close Session.
  2. For each foreign cash row, type the counted amount (fields start at zero).
  3. For each foreign bank method and currency row, type the counted amount (fields start at the expected amount).
  4. Click Close Session.
  5. A difference is posted in that currency. Bank differences use Currency Difference Journal when it is set, otherwise the method’s journal.

Check an order

  1. Open the Point of Sale app.
  2. Click Orders, then Orders.
  3. Open the order.
  4. Open the Payments tab.
  5. Read Tender Currency, Amount in Currency and Tender Rate.

Check a closed session

Journal Items requires accounting read access. In Community, this is Show Accounting Features - Readonly (also included in Show Full Accounting Features); in Enterprise, Accounting: Read-only, Bookkeeper or Accountant includes it. If the button is missing, ask your administrator to check your access.

  1. Open the Point of Sale app.
  2. Click Orders, then Sessions.
  3. Open the closed session.
  4. Click Journal Items.
  5. The list includes the session entry, the cash and bank payments, the exchange difference and the foreign bank difference entry.

Sales Details

  1. Open the Point of Sale app.
  2. Click Reporting, then Sales Details.
  3. Click Print.
  4. Point of Sale currency totals exclude foreign tenders.
  5. Under Foreign currencies, read per currency the tendered amount, the Point of Sale amount, the rate, and the counted cash and bank amounts.

Known issues / Roadmap

Foreign banknotes in the cash drawer are valued at the company rate of each movement. The module does not revalue a currency that stays in the drawer.

A Point of Sale has one cash register. Foreign cash is counted on that register, per currency. A second cash method cannot take a foreign tender.

The Point of Sale currency must be the company currency. A foreign list is rejected otherwise.

A payment terminal cannot take a foreign tender. The terminal sends an amount in the Point of Sale currency.

A price list in the foreign currency, used instead of the tender rate, is a separate module. It overrides Order.getForeignDue, Payment.computeAmountFromForeign and pos.payment._foreign_amount_matches_rate.
