POS Payment Method Fees

This module records the financial cost of POS bank payment methods (acquirer, gateway or platform fees) without changing the order, its taxes or the amount paid by the customer: fees are an internal financial cost.

  • Several percentage and fixed fee rules per payment method, cumulative.
  • Only payment methods with a bank journal can use transaction fees.
  • Fees are computed once per payment and rule when the order is validated, and stored in a fee ledger (Payment Fees) with a snapshot of the conditions used, so later configuration changes never alter them.
  • Posting at order validation (Immediate) or at session closing, in dedicated journal entries linked to the session and the payments.
  • Grouped or detailed journal items, always one journal entry per session, company, journal and accounting date.
  • Multi-currency: fees are converted at the payment date, like the standard session closing.
  • A posting failure never blocks a sale nor the session closing: the fee is left in error and can be posted again.

Configuration

  1. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Payment Methods and open a payment method with a bank journal.
  2. Check Use Transaction Fees: the Transaction Fees tab appears.
  3. Choose the Fee Grouping Policy, used for the fees posted at session closing:
    • Grouped: one expense journal item per expense account.
    • Detailed: one expense journal item per fee.
  4. Add one or more fee rules:
    • Fee Type and Amount: percentage of the payment amount, or fixed amount per transaction in the Fee Currency.
    • Apply On Refunds: by default refunds generate no fee and original fees are never reversed. When checked, refunds generate the fee computed on the absolute refunded amount.
    • Posting Policy: Immediate (when the order is validated) or At Session Close.
    • Fee Account (expense), Posting Journal (bank or miscellaneous) and Counterpart Account.

Reconciliation with the bank settlement

Leave the Counterpart Account empty to use the outstanding account of the payment method (or the company Outstanding Receipts Account), which is the account debited by the standard session closing for bank payments. The fee is credited there, so the balance left is the net amount the bank settles. For example, a card sale of 100 with a fee of 2:

Account Debit Credit
Outstanding (session) 100  
Bank fees (fee entry) 2  
Outstanding (fee entry)   2

The bank statement line of 98 is reconciled against both outstanding journal items. With the Immediate policy the fee reaches the outstanding account before the session closing books the payment, so its balance is temporarily negative. Another counterpart account must be reconcilable and must match the settlement circuit of the bank; this cannot be checked by the module.

Usage

  1. Sell in the POS with a payment method using transaction fees. Fees are computed when the order is validated; draft orders do not generate fees.
  2. Fees with the Immediate policy are posted right away; the others when the session is closed, whatever the way it is closed.
  3. Follow the fees in Point of Sale > Orders > Payment Fees or from the Fees button of the session.
  4. If a fee cannot be posted (e.g. deprecated account or locked period) it is left in Error with the reason and its accounting date is kept. Fix the cause and click Retry (POS or accounting managers), or close the session, which retries the fees in error.
  5. Disabling the fees of a payment method, or changing its rules, only affects new payments.
